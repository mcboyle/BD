## Column rules
- **routes moved:** count of url_map rules whose defining file changed this cut (0 for
  the kernel/helper cut; >0 for domain blueprints).
- **app.py before→after:** `wc -l bulk_downloader/app.py` immediately before and after.
- **snapshot sha:** `python3 tools/route_map_snapshot.py | sha256sum` from the extracted
  zip post-cut. MUST equal the baseline (`d92ccf3d8e05`) for every interior row.
- **version:** the `bd-cut` 3-part result.
- **on-stash:** the `capture.sh --workers=90 --summary` line (pass/fail/skip).
- **date:** ISO, the day it landed on stash.

## Re-freeze policy
Re-generate `route_map_baseline.txt` (and update `_BASELINE_SHA` in the test) at exactly
two points: **F5.1-open** (done — d92ccf3d) and **F5.1-close** (the intentionally-final
surface, if the close intentionally changes any route). Never mid-arc — a mid-arc
re-freeze silently defeats the gate.
