### Completeness contract (what `template_inventory` scores)

Each present field adds its weight to a 0-100 completeness score:

- `api_base`: 15
- `download_trigger`: 15
- `row_selectors`: 15
- `resolutions`: 15
- `login`: 10
- `player`: 10
- `quality`: 10
- `network_patterns`: 10

Promotion gate (mirrors `promote_template.py`): `(download.trigger | download.row_selectors | download.button)` + non-empty `resolutions` + no blocked terms.

### Status values

- `enabled` — reviewed/active; the registry loads only these.
- `draft_requires_review` / `draft_review_required` — draft/not-ready (both spellings are present in the codebase; tools accept either).
- `review_ready` — a normalized candidate that passes the gate.
